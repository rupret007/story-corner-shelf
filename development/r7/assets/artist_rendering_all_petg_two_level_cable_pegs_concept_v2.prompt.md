## Final targeted edit

> Starting from the first cable-peg concept, remove the peg from each of the
> two columns immediately adjacent to the inside 90-degree corner on both
> shelf levels: the last long-wall column and the first return-wall column.
> Those four corner-adjacent column faces remain clear. On every remaining
> pegged column, show a subtle narrow black PETG collar band around the
> lower-middle trunk so the peg reads as a removable clip-on accessory rather
> than part of the structural corbel. Keep exactly four representative neatly
> coiled cables on non-corner pegs. Preserve all other geometry, framing,
> lighting, materials, shelf levels, 6 + 3 bay composition, doors, and room
> details. Add no shelves, rails, bins, people, labels, logos, outlets, tools,
> or load claims.

The two corner/run-start positions are intentionally excluded from the
straight-out hook layout because their cross-arm service-swept plan reserve is
only 4.2325 mm. The current R7 concept therefore supports nine normal-facing
collar-hooks per shelf level. A future handed tangential corner hook would need
its own exact Boolean/service qualification.
