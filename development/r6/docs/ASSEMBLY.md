# Assembly — two independent L levels

> **Qualification gate:** This sequence documents the intended mechanics. Do
> not perform an overhead wall installation while r6 remains experimental,
> unrated, or fitted with solid development wall plates. Production bores are
> blocked until the real fastener, driver, wall finish, framing, utilities, and
> wall mockup have been verified. No tested load rating exists.

The shelf body, supports, joinery, keeper strips, facade, pins, and cross-keys are
printed black PETG. The only nonprinted installed items are suitable metal structural
screws with integral heads or compatible metal washers into verified wood
studs or purpose-installed blocking.

Use the governing [plan](generated/drawings/plan_layout.svg),
[exploded joinery](generated/drawings/exploded_joinery.svg),
[crown sequence](generated/drawings/crown_assembly_sequence.svg),
[corner detail](generated/drawings/corner_ownership_clearance.svg), and
[two-level layout](generated/drawings/two_level_vertical_layout.svg). The
artist rendering is visual intent only; drawings, configuration, schedule, and
physical labels govern.

## Before beginning

Complete these gates:

- all fields in [MEASUREMENT_WORKSHEET.md](MEASUREMENT_WORKSHEET.md);
- actual support stations verified over suitable framing or blocking;
- production fastener geometry regenerated and wall-mockup tested;
- exact printer, nozzle, plate, black PETG, slicer settings, and orientations
  qualified;
- one worst-case bay and the full corner dry-fit completed;
- the full [TEST_PROTOCOL.md](TEST_PROTOCOL.md), including 90 days sustained
  load and 72 hours recovery, accepted under written criteria;
- final generated manifest, validation, and parts schedule reconciled;
- wall clear, empty, and safely accessible with all power/utility precautions
  in place.

Never assemble by scaling parts, drilling development meshes, sanding away a
bearing shoulder, forcing a fit, or adding adhesive. Tune a configured
clearance, regenerate, and repeat the affected test.

## Identify and stage one level

Stage only one level at a time. A complete level has 258 installed printed
objects: 225 chassis/joinery/retention objects and 33 removable ornament
objects. Coupons and spares are separate. Confirm every logical ID against the
final parts schedule.

The principal per-level counts are:

- 18 cassettes and 18 handed arcade halves;
- 11 X-corbels with integral full-width bearing caps and 22 cassette locks;
- 36 cassette-top and 18 spring positive quarter-turn cross-keys (the retained
  inventory family names still call these “wedges”);
- 48 diaphragm keys, 9 fixed-crown diaphragm keeper strips, and 9 fixed crown
  ties; no separate front key is used at the seven floating pier seams;
- 18 indexed crown-interface pins: 9 underside keeper pins and 9 visible-front
  tie pins;
- 9 crown bridges and 9 fixed-right crown pins;
- 18 ornament carriers, 11 pier overlays, 2 ordinary end caps, and 2 corner
  finish pieces.

Keep through/return, left/right, start/interior/end, fixed/floating,
and lower/upper labels separated. Integral top and spring tenons are features of
the arcade halves, not additional loose parts.

## Assembly order: upper level first

The upper level must be installed and fully service-checked before the lower
level. The provisional shelf-top offsets are +33 in and +12 in above the outlet
top, but field-verified elevations govern. Do not connect the levels to one
another.

### 1. Establish the level and stations

1. Transfer one common laser datum across both walls.
2. Mark the verified shelf-top elevation and the generated support centers.
3. Confirm the long arm passes through the corner and the return structure
   begins at the regenerated datum beyond the complete facade service sweep.
   Confirm wall bow, angle, the 1.2 mm locked all-solid gap, 2.0 mm relieved
   visible-base gap, 18.8 mm chassis-to-return structural clearance, outer
   clearances, at least 30.2325 mm nominal cap-to-corbel clearance, and the
   separate 21.8325 / 14.6325 / 8.6325 / 4.2325 mm chassis / integral-boss /
   locked-facade / service-swept-facade reserves against the regenerated
   drawing.
4. Re-scan every production fastener path for utilities. Confirm every station
   lands on the verified framing/blocking represented by the wall mockup.
5. Place no structural reliance on the outlet box, wall finish, corner trim,
   or corner rosette.

### 2. Install the 7 + 4 independent X-corbels

1. Work from the regenerated production drilling template, not the nominal
   stations in a development file.
2. Attach the seven through-arm and four return-arm X-corbels with the tested
   screw/head-or-washer arrangement. Each corbel requires the configured
   minimum of three structural screws unless the qualified production design
   explicitly requires more.
3. Tighten only to the qualified seat condition. Do not crush PETG or draw a
   bowed wall plate flat by screw torque.
4. Verify plumb, integral-cap bearing elevation, front projection, and wall
   contact at every station. Stop if any screw, plate, wall finish, or framing
   response differs from the mockup.

### 3. Inspect the integral bearing caps

1. Confirm each corbel's 48 mm-wide cap is an integral printed feature, joined
   to the 28 mm X-body by the continuous full-width cap. There is no separate
   saddle or saddle pin in the active baseline.
2. At each of the seven internal supported-pier seams, identify the tight
   fixed-side locator and the elongated floating-side locator/lock seats. The
   latter must preserve the qualified 1.2 mm axial travel.
3. Confirm the cap spans beneath all three floating-pier diaphragm-key mouths
   throughout that travel. It is their positive gravity trap; do not add a
   keeper, glue, or fixed front key at a floating pier.
4. The active baseline has no stitch rails, rail pins, or run-end ties. Do not
   add optional study parts or infer load credit from them.

### 4. Build and lock the cassette train

1. Place the 12 through cassettes and 6 return cassettes in logical-ID order.
   The continuous skin is the shelf top; the coffered structure faces down.
2. Seat each cassette on its integral-cap locators without
   forcing it against either wall.
3. Install two cassette locks at every support. Keep each lock inspectable.
4. At each of the 16 internal cassette seams install three diaphragm bowtie
   keys at the generated depth stations.
5. At each of the 9 crown seams, lift the widened rear tongue of one left-owned
   diaphragm keeper 6.8 mm through its bottom entry, then slide the strip 4 mm
   rearward into the narrowed capture throat. Insert the keeper-reach indexed
   pin from the open underside, turn it exactly 90 degrees, and seat its index
   nub. The left ownership keeps it opposite the fixed-right crown-pin ear. The
   strip positively covers all three key mouths without preloading them and
   receives zero shelf-load or splice credit.
6. Insert one fixed front entablature tie at each crown from the visible front
   toward the rear and seat its hard stop. Insert its flat-bar indexed pin from
   the visible front, push it toward the wall, turn it exactly -90 degrees, and
   seat its positive-e index nub. The bar must remain visible and serviceable;
   friction or later arch contact is not retention.
7. At each of the 7 supported pier seams, confirm the three diaphragm-key
   pockets, cap locator, and both cassette-lock slots preserve the generated
   axial travel. Do not add the
   deleted front-entablature alignment key; its underside access collided with
   the final-X receiver path.
8. Recheck the corner: the through cassette owns the corner; the return remains
   independently supported and separated by the regenerated gap.

### 5. Lift each arcade half at final X

This is a vertical-lift assembly. **Do not slide an arcade half along the
run.** The rejected longitudinal path collides at the crown and can deadlock.

For each handed half:

1. Before ornament is installed, verify two cassette receivers plus one spring
   receiver are open from below and their visible-front cross-key gates are
   free of debris.
2. Hold the half at its final run/X coordinate. Align all three tenons at once.
3. Lift straight upward. The two top tenons must enter their open-bottom
   cassette receivers while the spring tenon enters its pier receiver. Entry
   and installed run coordinates are identical.
4. Seat both broad cassette compression pads and the broad spring shoulder
   against their positive stops. Do not use a cross-key to pull an unseated
   shoulder into place.
5. Insert two cassette cross-keys and one spring cross-key from the visible
   front with each crossbar vertical. Translate each key 27.2 mm toward the
   rear, turn it clockwise exactly 90 degrees in its chamber, and release the
   exposed flex tab into the unique locked notch.
6. Confirm each dog is visibly indexed and each shoulder still bears on its
   broad stop. Cross-keys prevent withdrawal only; they receive zero vertical
   shelf-load credit and may not pull a mis-seated frame into place.
7. Confirm at least 75 mm straight service access and a visible pull/flex
   feature for every cross-key.
8. Repeat for the mating half without crossing or forcing the crown seam.

### 6. Insert each crown bridge upward

1. From the open underside, align the rear crown bridge with both open-bottom
   keyways.
2. Move it straight upward at least through its 48 mm approach until its
   integral shoulders meet the hard positive stop.
3. Insert the single accessible pin front-to-rear through the fixed **right**
   half in double shear and engage its printed positive keeper.
4. Do not add a second fixed pin. Do not insert the bridge from above. Do not
   rely on friction as the keeper.
5. Confirm the bridge can later move down and the pin can later be pulled with
   cassettes and structure in place. The pin is anti-drop/reverse-slide
   retention only and receives zero shelf-load credit.

Repeat for all nine crowns.

### 7. Install isolated ornament last

1. Confirm every structural retainer remains reachable through the generated
   full-depth oculus or after removal of its carrier/overlay.
2. Fit the 18 half-arch carriers, 11 six-flute pier overlays, 2 ordinary end
   caps, and 2 corner finish pieces. The floating return-corner piece's
   zero-credit 4.4 mm cantilever is installed last at the corner.
3. Keep the right visual keystone fixed to its carrier and the left side
   floating across the crown seam.
4. Fix the nine-petal corner rosette only on the through finish; let the return
   mate float. Do not create a structural corner tie.
5. Verify the unloaded ornament-to-chassis isolation gap and the qualified
   deflection clearance. Ornament receives zero structural credit.
6. For corner service, fully unload the shelf and remove the floating return-
   corner finish first. Keep it absent throughout the fixed through rosette's
   complete axial insertion and gravity-release sweeps; reinstall it last.

### 8. Service-check before the next level

Verify all 11 wall attachments and integral caps, 22 cassette locks, 48 diaphragm
keys, nine keeper strips, nine front ties, 54 half-frame quarter-turn
cross-keys, 18 indexed keeper/tie pins, nine crown pins, floating travel, end reserves,
corner clearance, and removal paths. Record photographs and unloaded baseline
deflection. Only then repeat the complete process for the lower level.

## Initial loading and inspection

Do not improvise an installed proof test. Follow the accepted loading plan and
keep people and pets outside the fall zone. Apply only the qualified load
distribution, check the recorded deflection and joint baselines, and stop on
any sign listed in [SAFETY.md](SAFETY.md). The shelf is not a step, ladder,
handhold, or impact surface.

## Disassembly and service

Fully unload both levels and remove stored items. Service the lower level first
if it obstructs access to the upper. For each level:

1. remove isolated ornament and expose all cross-key oculi;
2. pull the single fixed-right crown pin and lower each crown bridge;
3. release each exposed flex dog, turn the two cassette and one spring
   cross-key per half back to the vertical entry index, and withdraw them;
4. lower each arcade half vertically at its final run coordinate;
5. push, quarter-turn, and withdraw each visible-front tie pin; remove the nine
   front ties. Push, quarter-turn, and withdraw each underside keeper pin;
   slide each keeper 4 mm forward and lower its rear tongue;
6. lower the fixed/floating diaphragm keys, then remove cassette locks and
   cassettes;
7. support each X-corbel/cap while removing its wall screws.

No disassembly step may require a whole-half longitudinal slide, wall-side-only
tool path, above-cassette pin path, or removal under load. Quarantine any part
with whitening, permanent set, crushed bearing, hole growth, cracked layers,
or unknown damage; do not reinstall it based only on appearance.
